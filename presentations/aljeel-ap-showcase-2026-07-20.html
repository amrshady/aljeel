Asateel · Fleet & Transportation

The Asateel allocation engine

Asateel is an allocation problem: every transportation ride (JQ) must be resolved to the correct agency, cost center, division and GL account — across five regions — and split when a ride serves multiple agencies.

  • Reads the full multi-page invoice PDF plus the daily shipping report — ride → dispatch → customer → division.
  • Oracle SO_Detail export is the authoritative source for agency assignment (system of record).
  • Canonical JQ normalization so every reference matches cleanly against Oracle.
  • Multi-agency rides are split evenly with finance-approved cost-center inheritance.
  • Runs automatically on AP approval — no manual trigger, picks up engine fixes on the next run.

Trust & Controls

Nothing enters review unvalidated

Before a Jawal submission reaches AP, the platform validates the entire evidence pack — files, structure and approvals — and tells the supplier exactly what to fix in plain language.

Gate A — File integrity

  • No empty or corrupt files
  • File content must match its extension (no disguised files)
  • PDFs must open; workbooks must be valid Excel

Gate B — Structure & approvals

  • Every ticket has a matching evidence folder
  • Every line carries the required approval (OPEX / email thread)
  • No orphan folders, no duplicate or malformed references

🗣️Supplier-friendly by design

Validation messages are written for the vendor, not the developer. When a large upload takes time, the supplier now waits and sees the real result — the exact folder or reference to fix — instead of a confusing technical error.

Shipped today: larger submit window for big evidence packs + clear bilingual (EN/AR) error messages. Validation strength unchanged.

📚Full audit trail

Every submit, approval, hold and rejection is recorded with who, when and what changed.
